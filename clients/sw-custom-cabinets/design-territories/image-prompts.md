## One Name, Real Work

A restrained, architectural editorial photograph of a real high-end custom
kitchen - rich walnut cabinetry, a waterfall-edge island, matte black
hardware - shot in soft natural daylight against a neutral white and
charcoal-gray interior. Generous negative space around the cabinetry rather
than a busy, staged, accessory-filled room. A small inset detail shot in one
corner shows close-up wood grain and joinery, quiet and unobtrusive. The color
palette is almost entirely neutral (white, charcoal, soft gray) except for
the warm real wood tones of the cabinetry itself - no gold, no warm "luxury"
color grading, no oversaturation. The composition feels architectural and
confident, closer to a design-magazine feature than a real-estate listing
photo or a sales brochure. Photographic realism, natural light, generous
whitespace even within the photograph's own composition. 16:9.

**What to look for:** does this feel like restraint (Medallion's actual
strength) or does it feel empty/cold? The balance to hit is "confident and
uncluttered," not "sparse." If it reads cold, the fix is usually more warmth
in the wood tones and light, not more decoration.
