## 2. Digital Footprint

### Website (sw-cabinets.com)
- Family-owned positioning stated directly, tone is plain and service-focused,
  not luxury-styled
- Photography: warm, natural-toned project photos - real work, not stock
- No pricing published (consultation-based, standard for this trade)
- **Confirmed: `702kitchens.com` is the same business**, not a separate
  company - same address (6764 B Preston Ave), same license (C6 #979326),
  branded as "Premium Bay Area Kitchens" instead of SW Custom Cabinets. This is
  a real finding, not just a hunch: two domains, two names, one business,
  competing with itself in search rather than consolidating authority under
  one identity - the exact keyword-cannibalization pattern this repo's own
  SITE-AUDIT process flagged for Coda Construction. Worth asking directly why
  there are two, and which one (if either) they actually want to be found
  under.
- 702kitchens.com's copy also reads noticeably more "sales-generic" than
  sw-cabinets.com - phrases like "luxury estates" and an odd, oddly-specific
  "scientific core" line stand out as possible low-quality or auto-generated
  SEO copy, inconsistent with the plain, family-owned tone on the main site.
  Worth a direct look together before the visit if there's time - thin/
  auto-generated content across a second domain is itself a real SEO risk
  (Google can discount it), separate from the trust-signal risk above.
- Owner confirmed: Steven Francis Waybright ("Steve" in reviews) - matches the
  name customers reference directly in both positive and negative BuildZoom
  reviews.
- Google rating/review count not found via search in this pass - BuildZoom is
  the only review source confirmed so far. Worth a direct Google Maps check
  before or during the visit; a real Outscraper/GBP pull would need to run
  through the pipeline (costs a small API fee) if you want that on record.

## Primary business/digital problem (one sentence, for the visit)

A contractor with a large, genuinely high-end book of business is running two
separate, weak websites under two different names for the same license -
splitting his own authority in search and giving a prospect who lands on
either one a worse impression than the real work deserves.
