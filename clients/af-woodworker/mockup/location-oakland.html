Custom Cabinetry in Oakland

Oakland's housing stock runs older than a lot of the Bay Area, Craftsman bungalows, mid-century layouts, the occasional Victorian, so a lot of what we build starts by working around a kitchen that was never designed for the appliances or storage a modern household actually needs. We model the real space in 3D before anything is built, so the fit is exact, not estimated.

Most of the Oakland projects we've taken on involve working within an existing footprint rather than a gut renovation, older kitchens tend to have layouts and load-bearing walls that make a full reconfiguration expensive or impossible. That's exactly where the 2D/3D modeling step earns its place: seeing the real constraints before committing to a design avoids a costly redesign mid-build.
