ABOUT ELIAS
Who You Are Hiring
The owner is the one on your job site.
Coda Construction is owner-led by Elias Torrez, an East Bay remodeler who is on your job site, not just on your proposal.
Elias has remodeled homes across the East Bay since 2010, but his approach was shaped before he ever started Coda.
Before construction, he worked in corporate America, where he learned how to run a process: plan clearly, organize teams, manage moving parts, communicate often, and finish what was started.
That structure matters in remodeling.
Because most projects do not fall apart from the work alone. They fall apart from poor communication, unclear scope, loose timelines, and contractors who disappear once the job starts.
Elias built Coda to do it differently.
Construction was already in his family. His grandfather, uncles, and cousins were builders, and Elias grew up around the trade. He fell in love with the craft early, especially woodworking, building tables and cabinets by hand.
When he returned to construction, he brought both sides with him: real craftsmanship and a disciplined process.
The turning point came after hiring a contractor for his own home and being let down by the runaround, unclear communication, and lack of follow-through. He knew homeowners deserved better.
So Coda was built around a simple promise: Clear scope. Clear communication. Quality work. No surprises halfway through.
On every project, Elias walks the space with you, defines the full scope in writing, and makes sure the work is planned before it starts.
That is why Coda clients come back for their next remodel and send their neighbors.
