Custom Casework & Millwork in San Francisco

San Francisco's building stock runs from Victorians to mid-century commercial spaces to brand-new buildouts, often within the same few blocks. Real work here means fitting custom millwork into whatever era of building it's going into, not one standard approach applied everywhere.

Most San Francisco commercial projects come with real constraints a suburban buildout doesn't have to plan around: tighter footprints, older buildings with their own structural quirks, and a design and permitting process that moves differently than a ground-up new build. Being involved from the planning and specification stage, not handed a finished drawing to execute, is what keeps those constraints from becoming surprises mid-fabrication.
