A private club bar has to survive a full service night after night without ever looking like it's built for punishment. The walnut counter and back-bar millwork were built to carry that load, real daily use, real volume, while still reading as considered joinery rather than a commercial fixture.

Involvement started at the planning and specification stage, not after the design was already finalized elsewhere. That's the same process every Arnold + Egan project runs on, whichever sector it's built for: design and detailing first, fabrication in the San Francisco shop, installation carried through by the same team, one point of responsibility from the first drawing to the finished install.
