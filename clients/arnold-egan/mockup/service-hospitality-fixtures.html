Services

Commercial & Hospitality Fixtures

Bars, restaurants, and private clubs, where the millwork has to hold up to a full service night after night without ever reading as a commercial fixture rather than considered joinery.

What's included

  • Involved from the planning and specification stage
  • Bar counters, back-bar millwork, and banquette seating
  • Built to hold up to real daily service volume
  • In-house fabrication in the San Francisco shop
  • Modern or traditional detail, same standard either way
  • Installation carried through by the same team

Real locations you can walk into and see the quality yourself: Champion's Bar at the Olympic Club, Resolute, Blue Bottle Coffee, Twitch.

Questions before you start

Do you work directly with our architect or designer?

Yes. Involvement starts at the planning and specification stage, working alongside whoever else is already on the project.

Can hospitality millwork actually hold up to daily service?

That's the specific standard this category is built to. Champion's Bar is real, ongoing proof, not a claim.

Do you handle installation, or just fabrication?

Both. The same team carries it from the shop to the finished, installed piece.
