## 2. Digital Footprint

**arnoldandegan.com/about read directly 2026-09-15.** Their own words:

- Identity: "Arnold & Egan is a Full-Service Casework & Millwork
  Manufacturing Company based in San Francisco, California."
- Service scope: "Commercial, Hospitality, Retail and Residential
  Clients" - matches the broad range already inferred from the photos.
- Process claim: capable of taking "any project from the Architectural &
  Planning stages and see it through to completion" - full-service,
  design-through-build, not just fabrication-to-spec.
- Quality/differentiator line: "High-Quality Fixtures that are
  personalized to their unique specifications" - customization is the
  stated core value, not just an implied one.
- No explicit tagline - positioning leans on capability breadth and
  decades of experience rather than a single mission line.
- Visual: black as the primary logo color, white/cream background, a
  minimal palette; typography reads as clean modern sans-serif, no
  ornamental flourish - notably NOT the traditional/heritage register
  their 89-year history might suggest, closer to AF Woodworker's modern
  register than to SW's ornate logo.
- Tone: professional, confident, understated - competence-and-scope
  focused, not promotional. Consistent with the one Instagram caption
  already on file ("Custom Millwork at Champion's Bar...").
