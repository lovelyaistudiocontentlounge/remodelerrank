How We Work

How We Build.

Good marketing doesn't start with a template.

We learn the business, inventory what already exists, figure out what customers need to see and understand, then build around it.

01
Walk the Job

Understand the business. See what we have.

Business · Customers · Projects · Goals · Photography · Reviews · Drawings · Credentials · Existing marketing

Don't organize it first. That's my job.

Output: Discovery + asset inventory + gap list

02
Draw the Plan

Decide what we're building, and why.

Customer journey · Messaging · Site structure · Search opportunities · Project strategy · Content plan

Output: Site plan + messaging direction + content plan

03
Prep the Materials

Turn the raw material into something we can build with.

Projects selected. Photos organized. Stories extracted. Reviews gathered. Credentials verified. Partners identified. Missing pieces filled.

Output: Build-ready content + asset library

04
Build

Put it together.

Copy · Design · Projects · Search · Mobile · Contact flow

And while the site is being built, the supporting content is being built with it.

05
Punch List

Check everything.

Copy · Projects · Credits · Mobile · Forms · Links · Search · Analytics · Technical QA

Measure twice. Launch once.

06
Turn the Lights On

Don't just launch it. Put it to work.

Website · Social · Project features · Partner amplification · Google · Email · Search

The website is live.

Now it can go to work.
