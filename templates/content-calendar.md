# 6-Week Launch Content Calendar

For new clients on Presence, Active, or Growth tiers. Starts the week of site launch.

**Visual rule:** Always pair content with beautiful project photography. Sage is the frame, the project is the hero. Never solid green backgrounds alone.

**No emoji anywhere.** No slang. No hype. Premium product.

---

## The 6 Pillars

Every content piece maps to one pillar. Rotate through them to build authority across all six pain points.

| # | Pillar | Core insight |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Projects as Content Goldmines | "I have great photos, now what?" |
| 2 | Better Leads, Not More Leads | "I'm sick of tire kickers" |
| 3 | The Google Gap | "My competitor shows up and I don't" |
| 4 | Trust Before the Estimate | "Homeowners need to trust me before they call" |
| 5 | The Referral Bridge | "Referrals are great but drying up" |
| 6 | The AI-Ready Contractor | "What even is AI search?" |

## Week 1 - Launch Week

**Theme:** Introduce the brand. Lead with the work.

| Day | Format | Pillar | Title / Concept | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Mon | Static post | 4 | "We build for the homeowners who do their research." | Hero project photo. Intro copy. No hard sell. |
| Wed | Carousel (3 slides) | 1 | Before + progress + after on one project | Show the transformation. Caption each slide with a specific detail. |
| Fri | Reel (30-45 sec) | 1 | "Great projects, bad photos? 5-minute cleanup" | High-virality piece. Show how lighting + angle changes everything. No voiceover required - text overlays only. |

**Friday reel notes:** Film two versions of the same finished detail (bad angle, harsh overhead light vs. good angle, natural window light). Cut between them. Text: "Same project. Same phone. 5 minutes apart." End card: "Your photos should be working for you." This is the highest-potential piece of the launch week.

## Week 2 - Proof of Work

**Theme:** Show the range. Build the portfolio in public.

| Day | Format | Pillar | Title / Concept | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Mon | Static post | 4 | A detail shot from a recent project + one sentence about what made it hard to get right | Specificity builds trust. "The tile setter was there 3 days for that pattern." |
| Wed | Carousel (4 slides) | 2 | "4 questions that filter out the tire kickers before the estimate" | Pillar 2. Questions like: Do you have a realistic budget range? Are all decision-makers available? Actionable and shareable. |
| Fri | Static or short video | 3 | "Why does [Competitor City] contractor show up and you don't?" | Pillar 3. Explain GBP optimization in plain language. |

---

## Week 3 - Authority Building

**Theme:** Position as the expert, not just the vendor.

| Day | Format | Pillar | Title / Concept | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Mon | Static post | 5 | "How to turn one referral into five" | Referral ask scripts. When to ask, how to ask. |
| Wed | Long caption post | 4 | "Here is what happens when a homeowner googles you before they call" | Walk through the trust signals: website, GBP, reviews, photos. |
| Thu | Carousel (5 slides) | 6 | DIY trap carousel - Give a Claude prompt, show why it still won't rank | High-virality. Slide 1: "I gave ChatGPT this exact prompt to write a contractor website." Slides 2-4: Show the generic output. Slide 5: "AI writes the words. Google ranks the signals. These are different problems." Do not bash AI - position Jennifer as the expert who understands both. |

**Thursday carousel notes:** This is the second highest-potential piece of the 6 weeks. The hook is authentic because it names the thing contractors are actually trying. The payoff is educational, not defensive.

## Week 4 - Social Proof

**Theme:** Let the clients do the talking.

| Day | Format | Pillar | Title / Concept | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Mon | Quote graphic | 4 | Featured client review | Pull the most specific, detailed review. Design it as a clean quote card with project photo background. |
| Wed | Reel (30 sec) | 1 | Project walkthrough - phone-filmed on site | Raw is fine. Show the scope of the job. Talk briefly about one challenge and how it was solved. |
| Fri | Carousel | 2 | "The estimate that never became a project - and what we changed" | Story of refining the qualifying process. Honest and specific. |

## Week 6 - Momentum + CTA

**Theme:** Convert the audience you have built.

| Day | Format | Pillar | Title / Concept | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Mon | Static post | 4 | "We have [X] spots open for [season] starts - here is who we are looking for" | Scarcity + qualification. Warm lead magnet. |
| Wed | Carousel | 1 | 6-month results: "What happened when [Client Name] started treating every project as a content piece" | Before/after of their online presence, not just the physical project. Requires 6 months of data. Skip if too early. |
| Fri | Strong CTA post | 2-4 | "One free call. 15 minutes. I will tell you exactly where your online presence is losing homeowners." | Clear, direct, no pressure. Link to Calendly in bio. Repost at month end. |

---

## Posting Notes

- Post to GBP weekly (minimum) - pull from this calendar
- Instagram: all 3 posts per week
- Facebook: Wed and Fri posts only (lower frequency, higher engagement on this platform for contractors)
- Stories: project progress photos daily - no planning needed, just document
- Repurpose: carousels -> PDF download for email list (Growth tier only)

## Content Assets Jennifer Needs Per Client

Before building the calendar, confirm:
- [ ] 20+ project photos processed and in WebP
- [ ] 2-3 before/after sets (for carousels)
- [ ] 5+ real client reviews to pull quotes from
- [ ] Owner willing to appear on video (even briefly)
- [ ] GBP claimed and verified
