# Client Intake — Trust & Conversion Lever Inventory

Use this during the onboarding call (60 min) or the free audit call (30 min) to map every lever available to pull.
Some levers already exist and just need to be surfaced. Others require a simple yes/no on whether the client is willing to add them.

The goal: leave no available trust signal undocumented and no conversion lever unasked about.

---

## 1. The People — Humans Visible to Clients?

The biggest missed opportunity on most contractor sites: "family-owned" with nobody visible.
If the owner or crew can be humanized, it becomes the strongest differentiator available.

| Question | Notes |
|---|---|
| Are you the face of the business? Will clients meet you personally? | If yes — get photos of the owner. This is the hero story. |
| Do clients regularly meet your crew on the job site? | If yes — crew photos + names are a trust signal |
| Is this literally family-owned — spouse, sibling, parent working with you? | If yes — name them. Show them. This is rare and compelling. |
| Would you be comfortable with a short video intro (30-45 seconds, phone is fine)? | Even low-production owner video outperforms stock imagery every time |
| Do you have any candid job site photos — you working, not just the finished result? | Process photos + humans together = premium trust signal |

**If the owner declines visibility:** note it. Do not manufacture a "family-owned" story if there are no humans to show. Lean into craft, process, and proof instead.

## 2. Licensing & Credentials

These are table stakes but most sites either hide them or don't have them.

| Question | Notes |
|---|---|
| CSLB license number? | Goes in footer, trust bar, schema. Required. |
| License status — active, in good standing? | Verify at cslb.ca.gov before any claim goes live |
| Fully insured? General liability + workers comp? | Yes/No. If yes, get the certificate. |
| Bonded? | Yes/No |
| Any specialty licenses? (electrical, plumbing sub-license, HIC) | Each one is an additional trust signal |
| OSHA certifications on crew? | Less common, high value if present |

## 3. Certifications & Associations

| Question | Notes |
|---|---|
| NKBA member? (National Kitchen & Bath Association) | Significant trust signal for kitchen/bath |
| NAHB member? (National Association of Home Builders) | Good for whole-home/ADU |
| EPA Lead-Safe Certified? | Critical for pre-1978 homes — a legal requirement and a selling point |
| BBB accreditation? What rating? | If A or A+ — use it prominently |
| Houzz "Best Of" or "Pro" badge? | If earned — display it |
| Angi "Super Service Award"? | If earned — display it |
| Any manufacturer certifications? (e.g., authorized dealer for a cabinet or countertop brand) | Signals product expertise, can unlock brand co-op materials |

## 4. Reviews & Social Proof — What Exists and What's Possible

| Question | Notes |
|---|---|
| Google reviews: count and rating? | Pull from GBP before the call |
| Yelp reviews: count and rating? | Pull from Yelp before the call |
| Facebook reviews: count and rating? | Check their FB page |
| Houzz reviews? | Check Houzz profile |
| Thumbtack / Angi / HomeAdvisor reviews? | Note platform and count |
| Do you have a review request process right now? | Most don't — this is usually Month 2 work |
| Would you be willing to text happy clients asking for a Google review? | Yes/No — if yes, builds the system in Month 2 |
| Do you have any video testimonials from clients? | Rare and powerful — ask |
| Do you have before/after photo sign-off from past clients? | Needed before using real project photos publicly |

## 5. Portfolio & Photography — What's Real and What's Ready

| Question | Notes |
|---|---|
| How many completed projects do you have photos of? | Rough count — doesn't need to be exact |
| Where do the photos live? (phone, Google Photos, website, IG) | Need to know where to pull from |
| Do you take before AND after photos on every project? | If not — start now. Give them the 3-shot protocol (before, during, after from same angle) |
| Do you know which photos are your own work vs. builder placeholders? | If unsure — photo tagging is Month 1 work |
| Do clients ever share their own photos of the finished project? | User-generated = highest trust |
| Have you had any projects featured in media, blogs, or home tours? | Rare but extremely valuable if it exists |

## 6. Conversion Levers — Willing to Add?

These require a decision from the client. Do not assume.

### Financing
| Question | Notes |
|---|---|
| Do you currently offer any financing options? | If yes — what provider? GreenSky, Synchrony, Hearth, HERO, other? |
| Are you open to offering financing? | The data: 30-40% of homeowners say financing availability influenced their contractor choice. Projects over $10K close faster with 0%-interest options. |
| Are you willing to apply for a GreenSky or Synchrony merchant account? (typically takes 1-2 weeks, no upfront cost) | If yes — flag as Month 2 priority |

**If they say yes to financing:** add it to the hero section, trust bar, and a dedicated section. "0% financing available" is a conversion lever most competitors in their market are not using.

### Response Time Guarantee
| Question | Notes |
|---|---|
| How fast do you typically return calls or texts from new leads? | Same day? Within 2 hours? |
| Would you commit to a "same-day response" or "we call back within 2 hours" guarantee on the site? | Only recommend if they can actually deliver it. A missed guarantee is worse than no guarantee. |

### Warranty
| Question | Notes |
|---|---|
| Do you offer a warranty on labor? What's the term? (1 year, 2 year, lifetime?) | If yes — make it prominent. Most contractors don't advertise this. |
| Do you pass through manufacturer warranties on materials? | If yes — document and display |

### Timeline Claims
| Question | Notes |
|---|---|
| What is the typical project duration for each service you offer? (kitchen, bath, ADU, etc.) | Get real ranges: "Kitchen remodel: 4-6 weeks from demo to punch list" |
| Do you have any specialty services that are notably faster? (tile work, cabinet painting, countertop swap) | If so — a timeline claim in the hero is a powerful differentiator |
| Can you commit to a project start date at signing? | If yes — "Projects start within X weeks of signed contract" is a promise competitors rarely make |

**Note on timeline claims:** Do NOT invent a timeline claim. Only use what the contractor can honestly commit to. A claim like "3-5 days" works for cabinet refacing but would be dishonest for a kitchen gut-and-rebuild.

### Showroom or Physical Space
| Question | Notes |
|---|---|
| Do you have a showroom, office, or physical location clients can visit? | If yes — photos + hours on the site. Dramatically increases trust for high-ticket projects. |
| Do you do in-home consultations? Free? | If yes — this is a CTA. "Free in-home estimate — we come to you" converts well. |

### Referral & Partner Network
| Question | Notes |
|---|---|
| Do you have ongoing referral relationships? (architects, interior designers, real estate agents) | If yes — mention it. "Referred by Bay Area designers and architects" is a credibility layer. |
| Do you get referrals from past clients? What percentage of your business is referral? | High referral % = social proof claim: "70% of our clients come from referrals." |
| Are you on any preferred contractor lists? (Houzz Pro, certain real estate teams, etc.) | List them |

## 7. Voice & Story — Required for Copy Differentiation

Generic contractor copy ("premium materials," "attention to detail," "stress-free process") is identical across every competitor. The differentiator is always specificity.

Ask these to mine the real story:

| Question | Notes |
|---|---|
| Why did you start this business? What made you leave whatever you were doing before? | Usually the best story is here |
| What is the one thing you do that most contractors don't? | Their answer — word for word — is the hook |
| What is the worst thing a homeowner can experience with a bad contractor? | Their answer = the pain language for the site |
| What do past clients say they were most surprised by? | Usually something they take for granted — document it |
| Is there a project you are most proud of? What made it hard and how did you solve it? | This becomes the hero case study |
| How do you handle it when something goes wrong on a job? | Shows process maturity — often the real trust signal |
| What type of project do you most want to be known for? | Shapes the niche positioning |

## 8. Intake Checklist — Confirm Before Ending the Call

```
[ ] CSLB license number confirmed (verify at cslb.ca.gov)
[ ] Photo inventory done — know what's real, what's stock
[ ] Owner visibility decision made — hero or behind the scenes
[ ] Financing question asked — yes/no decision on record
[ ] Warranty terms documented
[ ] Timeline ranges documented for each service
[ ] Review request willingness confirmed — yes = Month 2 priority
[ ] Voice/story questions answered — at least 3 specifics captured
[ ] Before/after photo permission process agreed on
[ ] Access grant doc sent (remodelerrank.com/onboarding/access)
```
