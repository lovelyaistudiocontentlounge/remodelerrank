Common Questions

What contractors ask before signing on

Can I just get a website without a monthly retainer?

Yes. The Launch package is a one-time site build with no ongoing commitment. You get a conversion-focused site, GBP setup, and core listings. Many contractors start here and add a retainer once they see results.

Do I have to sign a long-term contract?

No. We work month-to-month after an initial 3-month commitment. SEO takes time to work, 3 months is the minimum to see real movement. After that, you stay because it's working, not because you're locked in.

How long until I see results?

Most clients start seeing ranking improvements within 60 to 90 days. Lead volume typically increases meaningfully by month 3 to 4. We track everything and show you the numbers monthly.

What if I already have a website?

We audit it first. If your existing site has a solid foundation, we may be able to optimize it rather than rebuild. We'll tell you honestly which makes more sense for your situation.

Do you work with contractors outside Northern California?

Right now, no. We're focused exclusively on Northern California remodelers, cabinet shops, and specialty trades. That focus is what makes us good at this. We may expand later, but not yet.

What makes you different from a general marketing agency?

We only work with businesses that build things. Every template, every keyword list, every piece of copy has been built for this industry. A generalist agency learns your business on your dime. We already know it.
