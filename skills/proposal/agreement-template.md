### 1. Scope of Services

RemodelerRank will provide the following digital marketing services during the engagement period:

**Month 1 — Foundation**
- Google Business Profile audit, cleanup, and optimization
- Photo rescue — download and archive existing project photos from website and profiles
- Photo tagging — catalog confirmed real work vs. unverified or placeholder images
- Website technical audit with written findings report
- Onboarding kickoff call

**Month 2 — Content**
- 2 project pages built on client website (before/after format, SEO-optimized for service cities)
- 4 Google Business Profile posts
- Review request system setup
- Basic on-page SEO: city pages, service pages, meta descriptions

**Month 3 — Visibility**
- 2 additional project pages
- 4 additional GBP posts
- Monthly performance report
- Strategy call — results review and recommendations for continuation

Any services not listed above require a written addendum signed by both parties before work begins.

---

### 2. Compensation

Services for Months 1 through 3 are provided at no charge as part of a pro bono engagement. No payment is required or expected during this period.

At the conclusion of Month 3, both parties may elect to continue under a paid monthly retainer. If continued, rates will be outlined in a separate written agreement. There is no obligation for either party to continue.

---

### 3. Term and Termination

This Agreement begins on the date both parties sign and continues for three (3) calendar months unless terminated earlier.

Either party may terminate this Agreement with 14 days written notice. Upon termination, Client retains all work completed and delivered to that point. RemodelerRank retains no ongoing rights to Client's accounts after termination.

---

### 4. Client Responsibilities

Client agrees to:
- Provide access to all relevant accounts (Google Business Profile, website, domain, social profiles) within 5 business days of signing
- Make a designated contact person available for a maximum of 2 hours per month for calls and approvals
- Provide feedback on deliverables within 5 business days of receipt
- Confirm which project photos represent actual [Business Name] work before those photos are used in any marketing materials

Delays caused by Client may result in adjusted timelines. RemodelerRank is not responsible for missed milestones caused by lack of client access or feedback.
