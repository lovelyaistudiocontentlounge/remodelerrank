## Copy Formulas

### Formula 1 - Pain + Cost + Fix
> "[Pain statement]. [What it costs them]. [What we do about it]."

Example: "Your competitor shows up in Google Maps and you don't. The contractor in the top 3 gets 10x the clicks. We fix that."

### Formula 2 - Observation + Question
> "[Specific true observation about their situation]. [Question that makes them feel seen]."

Example: "Homeowners searched for a remodeler in Walnut Creek last night. Which contractor called you?"

### Formula 3 - Stakes First
> "[The dollar amount or loss]. [Why that number is real]. [What prevents it]."

Example: "One missed kitchen remodel is $85K+. That's more than a year of marketing. You can't afford an invisible website."

## Contractor Pain Language - Why It Matters to Them

Every piece of copy speaks to one or more of these five pains. Always name the pain AND the cost of the pain. Never just describe a feature.

### The 5 Contractor Pains

**1. Invisible on Google**
Pain: "My competitor shows up and I don't."
What it costs them: The contractor in the top 3 gets 10x the clicks. Every day they don't rank is a day homeowners call someone else.
Copy trigger: "Homeowners searched for a remodeler in [city] last night. Which contractor called you?"

**2. Website that doesn't convert**
Pain: "I have a website but it doesn't do anything."
What it costs them: Homeowners decide in 8 seconds. A bad site doesn't just look unprofessional - it actively sends them to a competitor.
Copy trigger: "Homeowners make a decision before they scroll."

**3. Referral dependency**
Pain: "I'm living referral to referral - I can't control it."
What it costs them: Feast and famine. No pipeline means laying off crew when referrals dry up. One slow quarter can undo years of work.
Copy trigger: "Referrals are great. You cannot control them."

**4. Invisible GBP**
Pain: "My Google profile is half-finished and I don't know what to do with it."
What it costs them: GBP is the first thing homeowners see. A half-finished profile loses jobs every single day - silently.
Copy trigger: "A half-finished GBP loses you jobs every day. Silently."

**5. Wrong jobs / tire kickers**
Pain: "I'm getting leads but they're the wrong ones - small budgets, unrealistic expectations."
What it costs them: Wasted estimates, wasted time, wasted crew days. The real cost is the $85K+ job they didn't quote because they were tied up on a $15K job that came in through a bad website.
Copy trigger: "Sick of $30K budgets for $160K projects?"

### The Stakes Line

Always be able to answer: "What does it cost them if this problem doesn't get fixed?"
The answer for Northern California remodelers: One missed kitchen remodel = $85K+. That is more than a year of marketing at any tier. Use this math when the copy needs weight.

### Voice Rules

- Speak to the contractor as a peer, not a vendor
- Name their specific city when possible
- Use their words: "tire kickers", "living referral to referral", "feast and famine", "the estimate that didn't close"
- No hype. No exclamation points. No "amazing" or "incredible"
- Honest grades, honest observations, honest copy - always
- One pain point per ad - never combine two ideas in one creative

## The 4 Ad Formats

### Format 1 - Identity + Pain

Best for: Paid Facebook/Instagram, GBP ad credits

Structure:
```
[IDENTITY LABEL - all caps, small, muted]
[Bold serif headline - 1 pain point]
[REMODELERRANK - all caps, small, bottom left]
```

Identity labels: NORTHERN CALIFORNIA REMODELER / GBP-INVISIBLE CONTRACTOR / REFERRAL-DEPENDENT REMODELER / NORTHERN CALIFORNIA KITCHEN + BATH CONTRACTOR

Pain headlines (use exactly as written):
- "Your competitor ranks above you. They are not better. They just show up."
- "Homeowners searched last night. They called someone else."
- "Referrals are great. You cannot control them."
- "Sick of losing the estimate because they could not find you online first?"

Image prompt template:
```
Square social media graphic, flat solid background color [HEX], white serif headline text reading "[HEADLINE]", small all-caps sans-serif label above reading "[IDENTITY LABEL]", small all-caps brand name "REMODELERRANK" bottom left, generous margins, no photos, no icons, no decorative elements, premium editorial design, minimal
```

Sizes: 1080x1080 (feed) / 1080x1350 (4:5 feed)

---

### Format 2 - Desire Checklist

Best for: Paid social, cold traffic

Structure:
```
[IDENTITY LABEL]
Do you want:
[checkmark] [item 1]
[checkmark] [item 2]
[checkmark] [item 3]
[REMODELERRANK]
```

Default checklist items:
- Homeowners who find you before they call anyone
- Projects that match your actual skill level
- A pipeline that works when referrals dry up

Image prompt template:
```
Square social media graphic, flat solid sage green background hex #4A6E5E, white text layout: small all-caps label "[IDENTITY LABEL]" at top, bold serif headline "Do you want:", then three checklist items each with a small square checkbox icon filled sage: "[ITEM 1]", "[ITEM 2]", "[ITEM 3]", brand name "REMODELERRANK" bottom left small all-caps, no photos no gradients no decorative elements, premium minimal design
```

Size: 1080x1080

---

### Format 3 - WARNING Hook

Best for: Cold traffic stories, vertical reels cover

Structure:
```
[WARNING badge - red]
[Bold serif headline - curiosity]
[1-2 line subtext]
[REMODELERRANK]
```

Copy options:
- Headline A: "Northern California homeowners are searching for your services right now." / Sub: "Are they finding you - or your competitor who ranked higher on Google?"
- Headline B: "You are about to see exactly where your business is losing jobs online." / Sub: "Free audit. 48-hour turnaround. No obligation."

Image prompt template:
```
Vertical social media story graphic 9:16, [BACKGROUND: warm off-white #FAFAF8 OR dark charcoal #2C2C2C], small bold red badge reading "WARNING" in all caps at top, large bold serif headline text reading "[HEADLINE]", smaller body text "[SUBTEXT]", brand name "REMODELERRANK" centered bottom in small all-caps [white or muted sage], no photos, editorial and premium, generous white space
```

Size: 1080x1920 (story) / 1080x1080 (feed square version)
