Shot notes, for whoever takes the real photos

Photo 1

Contractor tools

  • Cordless drill
  • Circular saw
  • Tape measure
  • Speed square
  • Clamps
  • Carpenter's pencil
  • Utility knife
  • Hearing protection

Surface: clean plywood or wood workbench.
Light: natural, directional, real shadows.
Wear: genuinely used, light believable wear, clean and cared for. Not new, not filthy, not staged like a hardware-store ad.
Avoid: visible manufacturer logos, sawdust styling, duplicated tools.

Photo 2

Marketing tools

  • Laptop
  • Camera
  • Notebook + pen
  • Printed project photographs
  • Project page / wireframe

Match Photo 1 exactly on surface, light direction, and color treatment, so these read as one matched pair, not two shoots.
Avoid: coffee-cup/flower lifestyle styling, pink accessories, neon app icons, fake charts, influencer desk aesthetic. This is a workspace that takes construction businesses seriously, not a "girl boss" flat lay.
